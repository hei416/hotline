# Introduction to WhatsApp
WhatsApp is a free application for smartphones. It allows people to:
- Send text messages
- Make voice and video calls
- Share photos, videos, and documents
- Send voice messages

## Why use WhatsApp?
- Most families in Hong Kong use WhatsApp daily.
- It helps elderly people stay connected with children, grandchildren, and friends.
- It is free as long as your phone is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data.
