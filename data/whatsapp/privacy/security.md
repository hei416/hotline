# Privacy and Security

## Who can see your profile
- Open WhatsApp > Settings > Privacy
- You can choose who sees your profile photo, last seen, and status:
  - Everyone
  - My Contacts
  - Nobody

## Blocking unknown numbers
- If someone strange sends messages:
  1. Open the chat
  2. Tap the number at the top
  3. Select Block Contact

## Safety tip
- Do not share personal details like bank accounts or ID numbers over WhatsApp.
- Be careful of messages asking for money or passwords.
